# Calculator

[![▶ Play now](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E2%96%B6%20Play%20now-in%20your%20browser-ff9f0a?style=for-the-badge)](https://donaldweatherly645-coder.github.io/calculator-generic/)

A fun browser calculator with a big collection of themed skins: classic styles, consoles, snacks and drinks, and games. Every skin hides little details, and a few hide easter eggs.

## How to use it

Click **▶ Play now** above. Nothing to install.

- 🎨 Pick a skin from the menu at the top, or press `[` and `]` to flip through them.
- ⌨️ Type numbers on your keyboard or click the buttons.
